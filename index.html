About the Contest

The BSS Pairs Coding Invitational is a collaborative programming competition where teams of two students work together to solve algorithmic and logical challenges.

The goal of the contest is to encourage problem-solving, teamwork, and creativity in coding.

Rules for Bayridge Pairs Coding Competition 2026

Before the competition:
Competitors should go to bit.ly/bayridgedata2026 and download the entire folder. This folder contains the datasets needed for all questions. The files are password-protected.

  • There are two divisions:
    • Junior Division: For beginners (Grade 9–10), designed to be more accessible.
    • Senior Division: Traditional contest format as in previous years.
  • The contest is 3 hours long (starting around 10:00).
  • Each division has 6 questions.
  • Teams must bring their own devices and power cords. Extension cords are strongly recommended.
  • External monitors are allowed, but space will be limited.

Allowed Resources

  • Official documentation (e.g., Java Docs, Python Docs)
  • Books and notes (physical or digital)
  • Programs already on the device before the contest

Not Allowed

  • No access to other websites (no Google, StackOverflow, etc.)
  • No AI tools (e.g., ChatGPT, Copilot, etc.)

Judging

  • Any programming language or IDE may be used; judging is based on program output only.
  • To submit, teams raise a paper and receive the password for the dataset.
  • Each problem includes 10 input sets; output must match all.
  • Each correct output = 10 points (max 100).
  • Perfect score bonus: +10 points if achieved on the first attempt (total 110 + time bonus).
  • No bonus if not perfect on first attempt.
  • Up to 10 attempts per question.
  • The most recent score counts (not the highest).
  • Time is recorded at submission for tie-breaking.
  • Teams keep datasets and outputs after each attempt for debugging.

After One Hour

If a team is struggling, they may declare themselves "out" (ineligible to win). At that point, they may use the open Internet and any resources except AI tools.
