About the Contest

The BSS Pairs Coding Invitational is a collaborative programming competition where teams of two students work together to solve algorithmic and logical challenges.

The goal of the contest is to encourage problem-solving, teamwork, and creativity in coding.

Contest Rules

  • Teams consist of two students.
  • Only one computer may be used per team (external monitors allowed).
  • Students may bring books and written materials.
  • Students may use programs they have written themselves.
  • Students may access official documentation for their programming language.
  • No communication with others during the contest.
